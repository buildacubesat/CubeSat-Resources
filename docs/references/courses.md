# Courses, Events and Educational Kits

Educational kits you can build against, structured courses from mission design to operations, and the recurring conferences and workshops where CubeSat practitioners present their work.

## Educational Kits

<!-- CSR-RESOURCES:START ref-courses-educational-kits -->
- **[MOSAIC: Modular Orbital Satellite for Advanced Innovation and Curriculum](https://www.mosaicsat.org/)** `Link` – Modular educational CubeSat platform ([SmallSat Conference paper](https://digitalcommons.usu.edu/smallsat/2025/all2025/108/))
- **[Artemis CubeSat Kit](https://sites.google.com/hawaii.edu/artemiscubesatkit/home)** `Link` – University of Hawaiʻi open educational CubeSat kit, with accompanying open textbook and power budget spreadsheets
- **[PyCubed](https://pycubed.org/)** `Link` – Open-source, radiation-tested CubeSat avionics board programmable in CircuitPython; widely used for teaching and flown on multiple missions
- **[PROVES Kit](https://github.com/proveskit)** `Link` – Open-source CubeSat kit developed for education, flown on multiple missions
- **[FlapKit](https://www.flapkit.org/)** `Link` – Open educational CubeSat kit

## Courses

<!-- CSR-RESOURCES:START ref-courses-online-courses -->
- **[KiboCUBE Academy Webinars](https://www.unoosa.org/oosa/en/ourwork/access2space4all/KiboCUBE_Academy_Webinars.html)** `Link` – UNOOSA/JAXA lecture series covering CubeSat engineering from mission design through subsystems to operations, with published slide decks and recorded video. Free and open access – one of the best structured free CubeSat courses available.
- **[A Guide to CubeSat Mission and Bus Design](https://pressbooks-dev.oer.hawaii.edu/epet302/)** `Link` – University of Hawaiʻi open textbook used as a full course, covering mission design, power budgeting, ADCS and communications with worked examples
- **[New Space Economy (EPFL, edX)](https://www.edx.org/learn/economics/ecole-polytechnique-federale-de-lausanne-new-space-economy)** `Link` – Introduction to the commercial space sector, useful context for anyone building a business case around a small satellite

## Events

<!-- CSR-RESOURCES:START ref-courses-events -->
- **[Small Satellite Conference](https://smallsat.org/)** `Link` – Organised by Utah State University and held each August in Salt Lake City, running since 1987 and widely regarded as the premier small satellite conference. Its [proceedings archive](https://digitalcommons.usu.edu/smallsat/) is freely accessible and is the single richest source of CubeSat engineering literature.
- **[Open Source CubeSat Workshop (OSCW)](https://community.libre.space/c/oscw/28)** `Link` – Annual workshop organised by the Libre Space Foundation, focused specifically on open-source approaches to CubeSat development. Talks are recorded and published.
- **[Cal Poly CubeSat Developers Workshop](https://www.cubesat.org/)** `Link` – Long-running workshop run by the originators of the CubeSat standard; the archive of past presentations is a valuable resource in its own right
- **[ESA Academy training session portfolio](https://www.esa.int/Education/ESA_Academy/Portfolio_of_ESA_Academy_training_sessions)** `Link` – Hands-on training courses for university students from ESA member states, including a recurring CubeSat Hands-On Training Week covering spacecraft systems, testing and operations
