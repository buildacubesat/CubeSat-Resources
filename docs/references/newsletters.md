# Newsletters

Newsletters and mailing lists that keep you current on CubeSats, small satellite missions and the wider space industry. Most are free, and a few well-chosen ones are the cheapest way to follow launches, technology, funding and events without reading everything.

## Space industry

<!-- CSR-RESOURCES:START ref-newsletters-space-industry -->
- **[Payload](https://payloadspace.com/)** `Link` – Mainly focused on industry and business news
- **[Moon Monday](https://jatan.space/tag/moon-monday/)** `Link` – Curated Moon news, highly recommended
- **[Rocket Report](https://arstechnica.com/newsletters/)** `Link` – A classic. Focused on launches, with witty and pertinent commentary by Eric Berger
- **[The Journal of Space Commerce](https://www.exterrajsc.com/)** `Link` – Business news focus
- **[Planetocracy](https://planetocracy.org/)** `Link` – Mainly focused on human expansion into the solar system
- **[The Space Investor](https://thespaceinvestor.us5.list-manage.com/subscribe?u=4f29c5aca912733d639838bbb&id=4e877bd359)** `Link` – Good space industry health check, even if you don't invest
- **[Europe in Space](https://europeanspaceflight.substack.com/)** `Link` – A good one if you care about European space activities
- **[Open Lunar Foundation](https://www.openlunar.org/)** `Link` – Academia-focused, but important for cislunar development
