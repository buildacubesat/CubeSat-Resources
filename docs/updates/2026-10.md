# October 2026

A round of new tools and features were added: Engineering notebooks are now embedded on some of the subsystem pages, derating got a proper treatment across five pages sourced from ECSS-Q-ST-30-11, and the site can be cited with a DOI. The glossary grew by 18 entries along the way.

## Engineering notebooks

The marimo notebooks for link budget, power budget, optical payload and orbital lifetime are embedded as collapsible sections on the pages that use them. They are work in progress and meant for planning estimates. The "Tools" page keeps the links.
