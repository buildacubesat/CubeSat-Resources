    About CubeSat Resources¶

    Welcome to CubeSat Resources – a curated collection of links and resources for anyone interested in building CubeSats, from first-time makers to experienced developers.

    This site is a side project that arose from the Build a CubeSat project (website, YouTube, Codeberg). It's a community-driven collection of useful material from across the web: documentation, videos, open hardware projects, papers, and tutorials. It's meant to help you find reliable information and inspiration, not to serve as a step-by-step course.

    All content is open-source and community-curated, released into the public domain under CC0 1.0.

    Contributions are Welcome

    This project thrives on shared knowledge and collective effort – everyone is invited to contribute, suggest additions, or share useful finds:

    • Submit your contribution here: Google Form
    • ... or contribute to the GitHub repo

    What You'll Find Here¶

    • Getting Started – Basic concepts, project phases and milestones, further reading, relevant communities.
    • Development – Systems engineering and every subsystem (structure, power, avionics, software, communications, ground segment, attitude control, propulsion, thermal, payload), launch safety, integration and testing, launch, and tools.
    • References and Resources – Glossary, standards and deployer manuals, books, papers, missions, courses, communities, videos, websites and newsletters.
    • Updates – Monthly logs of changes and additions to the site.
